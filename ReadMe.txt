# Sanctions Network Monitoring Project

Early-stage research project exploring how network science and data science can be used to identify, visualise, and stress-test potential sanctions evasion corridors.

This repository is a working build. The scope, methods, datasets, and research question are expected to evolve as the project develops.

## Project Aim

The aim is to investigate whether network-based modelling can help detect or predict the emergence of indirect financial, trade, or logistics pathways used to route around sanctions pressure.

Initial focus areas may include corridors involving:

- Russia-linked trade rerouting
- China-adjacent supply chains
- Iran-linked intermediary jurisdictions
- Crypto and alternative settlement rails

## Working Research Question

> Can network-based modelling identify emerging sanctions evasion corridors under geopolitical and regulatory pressure?

This question is provisional and will likely be refined as the project develops.

## Why This Matters

Sanctions rarely stop flows outright. They often redirect them through intermediary jurisdictions, alternative payment systems, shell entities, logistics hubs, and commodity channels.

This project treats sanctions evasion as a network problem rather than a simple compliance checklist.

## Planned Features

- Build graph models of country, sector, entity, and trade-flow relationships
- Identify high-centrality intermediary nodes
- Visualise rerouting patterns over time
- Simulate disruption or enforcement pressure on specific corridors
- Develop an interactive dashboard or map-based briefing tool
- Explore the role of crypto and alternative payment systems where data permits

## Current Status

This repository is in the initial research and prototyping phase.

Current priorities:

1. Define the first target corridor
2. Identify usable public datasets
3. Build a basic graph model
4. Document assumptions and limitations
5. Create an early visual prototype

## Possible Data Sources

Potential sources may include:

- UN Comtrade
- IMF Direction of Trade Statistics
- World Bank datasets
- OFAC sanctions lists
- EU sanctions lists
- UK OFSI sanctions lists
- FATF reports
- Public blockchain datasets
- Open-source reporting and trade intelligence

Dataset selection is not final.

## Methodology

The project will likely use a combination of:

- Network analysis
- Centrality measures
- Community detection
- Anomaly detection
- Time-series comparison
- Scenario simulation
- Geospatial visualisation

Methods are subject to revision as the research question becomes more precise.
